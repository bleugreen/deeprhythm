# v0.8 metrical-level correction research

## Decision

Ship the tempo-balanced fine-tuned model without a postprocessor. On canonical held-out folds it raises 4%-tolerance Acc1 by **21.38 points on GiantSteps**, **29.10 points on Ballroom**, and **3.19 points on GTZAN**. The onset-periodicity corrector helps the biased 0.7 GiantSteps model, but harms Ballroom and GTZAN and makes no test prediction changes after fine-tuning.

Candidate weights live at weights/deeprhythm-v0.8-research.pth. They are deliberately not the package default: external-corpus composition ablations could not be completed with defensible audio rights, so this report does not turn a strong folds-only experiment into a production release by relabeling it.

## Error taxonomy and attribution

| Dataset | 0.7 half errors | Fine-tuned half errors | 0.7 correct | Fine-tuned correct |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| GiantSteps | 34 | **2** | 93 | **121** |
| GTZAN | 46 | **31** | 216 | **226** |
| Ballroom | 38 | **5** | 80 | **119** |

A low-tempo prior selected at 90 BPM raises combined validation Acc1 from 63.75% to 72.17%, but cannot distinguish resolution from dataset convention. Classifier top-k mass and clip disagreement reach only 64.72% and 64.40%. The learned onset-periodicity classifier reaches 66.02%. On test it improves 0.7 GiantSteps by 5.34 points but loses 1.28 on GTZAN and 0.75 on Ballroom.

Fine-tuning removes half-tempo errors consistently across domains. That is genuine octave-resolution evidence rather than only an EDM-is-fast prior.

Raw fine-tuned confidence remains poorly calibrated: held-out expected calibration error is 0.243 GiantSteps, 0.295 GTZAN, and 0.352 Ballroom. Calibration remains separate work.

## Latency

The fine-tuned network has the same architecture and inference cost as 0.7. On 578 held-out tracks, onset features take 1.735 seconds with eight workers, or **3.00 ms/track**. Warm serial cost over 20 preloaded tracks is **2.94 ms mean**, 2.91 ms median, and 3.29 ms p95. Audio loading is excluded because the intended pipeline reuses loaded audio. The recommended model-only design adds no runtime latency.

## External acquisition

The acquisition workstream audited every prioritized local and official source. No new external audio entered training:

- Extended Ballroom publishes metadata and a downloader, not audio or explicit source-audio training rights.
- GiantSteps audio consists of Beatport previews; the local repository does not grant training or redistribution rights.
- Hainsworth is publicly mirrored, but the mirror does not establish a license for copyrighted excerpts.
- ISMIR04, Harmonix, RWC, and Beatles could not be used with both verifiable alignment and defensible rights.
- FMA/MTG-Jamendo pseudo-labeling was not run because the required independent same-octave estimator ensemble was unavailable. DeepRhythm confidence was intentionally not substituted.

This blocks the planned +tier-1 and +tier-1+3 composition ablations. The measured attribution is therefore **tempo-balanced fine-tuning on canonical folds**, not increased data volume.

## Literature synthesis

Post-hoc octave selection depends on evidence outside a saturated tempo softmax. Schreiber and Müller's [2017 octave-correction study](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2017/paper/000125.pdf) motivates the periodicity reproduction. Their [single-step CNN work](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2018/paper/000151.pdf) describes the sweet-octave behavior observed here. Gkiokas et al.'s [periodicity-vector classifier](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2012/paper/000065.pdf) supports a small candidate classifier. Böck and Davies' [multi-task tempo/beat/downbeat model](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2020/paper/000035.pdf) shows why beat structure is stronger metrical evidence than a tempo posterior.

Evaluation follows the Acc1/Acc2 framing and cautions synthesized in [Music Tempo Estimation: Are We Done Yet?](https://transactions.ismir.net/articles/10.5334/tismir.43). Primary provenance sources are the [GiantSteps dataset](https://github.com/GiantSteps/giantsteps-tempo-dataset), [Extended Ballroom](http://anasynth.ircam.fr/home/media/ExtendedBallroom), and Sturm's [GTZAN fault analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1461).

## Reproduction and remaining work

Inference details retain per-clip peaks, aggregate peaks, and five candidate masses without changing legacy prediction JSONL. deeprhythm-taxonomy emits taxonomy and calibration tables. deeprhythm-correction performs train/validation-only selection and rejects test rows. deeprhythm.train provides manifest-hashed caching, balanced sampling, time-stretch/relabel primitives, and train/validation-only checkpointing.

Time-stretch augmentation is implemented but was not included in the frozen checkpoint because augmented manifest rows were not materialized. External composition and augmentation ablations remain future experiments, not implied results.
